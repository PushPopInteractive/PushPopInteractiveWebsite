Privacy Notice

Policy version 2026-09-21.1 · Effective September 21, 2026

Pushpop Interactive provides Fireside. For privacy questions or requests, contact pushpopinteractive@gmail.com.

Apple or Google sign-in supplies account identifiers and, depending on your choices, name and real or relay email. Supabase authenticates your account and stores your private library: player descriptions, stories, messages, private narrator conversations, notes, summaries, drafts and artwork, favorites and settings. Local device storage also keeps copies.

When you allow AI processing and request a reply or summary, Fireside’s server on Render sends the relevant conversation and story context through OpenRouter to the provider serving your selected model. This can include substantial history, private narrator exchanges, player descriptions, story setup, notes and summaries. Information you type may contain personal details. A model’s name does not necessarily identify the company hosting it.

Render hosts our credit and reply-recovery records, including completed reply text. Creator submissions, approved story versions, review decisions, reader access grants and reports are also stored on our server. Private copies of that database are backed up to Cloudflare R2. These services process technical and usage information to operate and secure the service. Public sharing is a separate action; AI processing does not publish your private stories to other players.

OpenRouter and model-provider retention and data-use practices depend on provider and configuration. We do not promise zero retention or that every provider excludes all training use. Their processing may occur outside your country. You can decline AI processing or stop future requests in Privacy settings.

We record policy versions, your permissions, age-screening result and server timestamps with your account. A birthday is used to calculate whether you are 18 or older and is not stored in the consent record. This is self-reported age, not identity verification. Consent records do not contain identity documents.

Library data, recovery records and backups are retained to provide account storage, reliable billing and recovery. There is not currently a single automatic expiry period for every record. Contact us for access, correction or deletion requests; legal recordkeeping and backup handling may affect timing. Signing out is not deletion. Access controls protect private records but cannot guarantee perfect security.
