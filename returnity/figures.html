Figures

People You Will Deal With

Alrathel is not populated by quest-givers. It is populated by people who have a job, an opinion about how it should be done, and usually a family member who disagrees.

At the guild counter

Lilia

Half-ElfGuild receptionist

Green-haired, capable and genuinely pleasant, Lilia works the reception counter at the main Adventurer's Guild branch in Eldrath. Every Returner in the world has met her, because every Returner in the world registers with her.

My, another Returner here to register? Let's get you squared away, then. Please fill out this form. Lilia, to everyone

The form records who you are and what you intend to do for a living. It does not change your body, test your strength, or assess your potential. Lilia is not appraising you. She is doing the paperwork, and she is fast at it.

The court at Eldrath

King Wilhelm

HumanKnight

A knight before he was a king, and it still shows in how he stands. He holds the throne in the royal palace at Eldrath and, with it, the leadership of the largest alliance in the world.

Queen Halwen Cor

Human

Wilhelm's wife and queen.

Edric Vaun

HumanChamberlain

Royal chamberlain. Runs the household, the schedule, and — as far as anyone below stairs can tell — the actual kingdom.

Captain Maelis Rook

HumanKnight

Commands the palace guard.

Selvara En

Half-ElfRoyal archivist

Studies the contradictory accounts of the hero who ended the Demon King's war a thousand years ago. Has not resolved whether that hero was a Returner, and says so plainly. See World History.

Lady Merra Dain

HumanCivic patron

A noble and a patron of the capital's public works. Not a member of the royal family, and will correct you if you imply otherwise.

The eight foreign delegations of the embassy district and the people who lead them are listed on the History page.

Names with a reputation

Shady Pete

Rogue

All-black clothing, a face mask, and a personality that has never let the truth get in the way of a better version of it. He turns up at the capital's guild hall and in the Shadow District, and he is not the sort of person you hire — he is the sort of person who decides, occasionally, to come along.

Xanatos

AmalgamNecromancer

Accepted in the dark elf city, unsettling to nearly everyone else, and the loudest voice in the world arguing that Amalgams are people. He speaks of death the way other people speak of a colleague. More Amalgams arriving has not softened his advocacy — it has simply given him more clients.

Bishop Samhain

HumanCleric

Senior figure at the capital's public temple. A gentle, stubborn healer who would far rather quietly preserve a life than publicly display power, which makes him unusual among senior clergy anywhere.

Gorndar Bravemane

BeastkinChief

Chief of Sunclaw Village and the highest authority among the Beastkin. A lion-maned warrior who still carries the sword and shield he was given. He stands above the village elder and above Rasha Sunstep, who speaks for Sunclaw at the capital and comes home for the herd count.

Captain Miquella

Ship master

Runs the Tidehaven–Eldrath route and the downriver work. Was becalmed once, a long way out, and prayed to Ogoth. She has kept a lamp burning ever since and does not care to discuss it.

Nella

SuccubusStallholder

Runs a stall at the Great Desert night market in Kaldron, and has for years. A demon living an entirely ordinary life, tolerated the way anyone unusual is tolerated — which is to say, mostly, and with the occasional idiot.

Stonewarden Brann

DwarfStonewarden

Underdeep authority on what is about to fall down and what merely looks like it. Rescue work under collapsed stone is the part of the job he does not talk about at dinner.

Tidecaller Sera

Wizard

Nobody drowns on her waters — she says this as a statement of policy rather than a boast. Works the western waterways out of Scalefen, and quietly hides people downriver who need hiding.

Nelly Saye

KemonoPet merchant

Sells adventuring companions in the capital market — weasels, cats, parrots, tortoises, and eleven other small creatures who will follow you across the world for reasons of their own.

Orvik Ironkeel

DwarfMaster shipwright

The capital's master shipwright. A dwarf who builds ships, which he is aware is funny, and does not need it pointed out again.

The crafters

Crafting in Returnity is not a skill you level. It is a person you have to find.

You harvest the raw material yourself, out in the world. Then you go and find the one crafter who knows what to do with it — the retired blacksmith, the alchemist hermit, the glassmaker working salt gypsum into lamp chimneys — and they turn you down, because they do not know you. So you do them a favour.

The material is the ticket. The crafter is the content. There are sixteen of them scattered across the world, and no two refine the same ore the same way.

See Harvesting and making things.

Around the cities

Every settlement is staffed by people with a specific job and a specific view of it. A small sample, to give you the shape of the thing:

Tidehaven — the port

  • Marra Orbeck, harbourmaster — runs berths, clearances and the harbour boat; the final word in a storm.
  • Juno Bask, coxswain — takes the boat out to wrecks on the bar. Crew off first, cargo never.
  • Nesbit and Ada Quarrow, salvagers — he works the Cliffwake ledges by charter as four generations have; she is the one who says the thing nobody wants said.
  • Corvin Ollocke, shipwright — repairs hulls, and refuses work he thinks will drown somebody.
  • Sister Ilm, harbour chaplain — keeps the drowned-list and the harbour's binding stone.
  • Denn Halloway, rogue trainer — badly disguised as a rope-splicer.

Winterhold — the north

  • Ansgar Vole, ice-road warden — marks and closes the ice road. Pulled the stakes himself once and was fined for it.
  • Sigrid Vole, garrison captain — commands the relief rotation. His sister. They argue in public.
  • Gudrun Stell, caravan master — thirty years on the ice; turns back when the road stops telling her where it is.
  • Astrid Oskarr, furrier — will not sell a southerner anything thin.
  • Sister Beya, healer — frostbite, mostly. Extremely blunt about how much of you is coming off.
  • Broc Halgrim, gate sergeant — counts everyone out and counts them back, and notices when the numbers differ.

Kaldron — the desert

  • Yusra Ballan, water master — sixty years of the city's water.
  • Yazir Ottal and Odwin Serro, night-market rivals — nineteen years undefeated at a contest with no judge, against a man nineteen years wrong about the salt and right about everything else.
  • Dune Sarai Ottal, well keeper — Yazir's daughter, ten years old, and already the one who checks the levels.
  • Milla Onn, innkeeper — beds by day, for people who travel at night.
  • Wren-of-the-Sand, druid trainer — insists the desert is not empty, merely private.

Scalefen — the wetlands

  • Speaker Ghuvel — speaks for Scalefen, and does not make excuses when the village has kept something badly.
  • Ozhek Tal-Marrin, retired sink diver — put his brother somewhere dry in the sink, and visited for forty years.
  • Yenna Tal-Marrin, diver — works Fenmouth Sink. Dives the short way now, and hates how short it is.
  • Ospen Vell, stilt-house keeper — was hidden here for years; now keeps the empty houses ready for whoever is next.
  • Grandmother Odo — keeps the binding stone on the only dry rise in the village.

Eight more settlements are staffed to the same depth: Deepforge, Sylvanor, Elandril, Val'thar, Sunclaw Village, The Light Below, Radiance, and Eldrath itself.

On travelling companions

Living Adventurers are native adventurers of this world — visibly local people, with their own lives, who will travel with you. They take a real place in your party, the same as anyone else.

Named story figures are a different matter. Nobody queues for Pete. No named character in Alrathel is a resource you line up to borrow, and none of them will follow you around permanently. A handful may join you for the length of a particular job — as a temporary extra hand your party does not need an open place for — and then they leave, because they have their own business to be getting on with.

If you log out during one of those stints, it ends. They were never yours to keep.
