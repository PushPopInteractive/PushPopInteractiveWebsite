Bestiary

Field Notes

Nothing in Alrathel attacks because you crossed an invisible line. Animals hunt when they are hungry or when they have the advantage. They defend their young and their kills. They give up when they are tired, or hurt, or a long way from home.

Which means almost everything out there tells you first. A territorial animal gives a readable warning. A social monster calls for help you can hear coming. A predator that has decided about you moves differently from one that has not.

These notes record what each creature does immediately before it commits, and what makes it break off. That is the useful part. Everything else is decoration.

Strength bands

Every creature carries a band describing what it is worth relative to its own region. A Dangerous creature in the Greenfield and a Dangerous creature in the Volcanic Lands are the same problem to very different people.

BandWhat you are looking at
MinorA weak nuisance, a prey animal, or a low-risk target on your own.
CommonAn ordinary fight, alone, at the region's level.
DangerousA hard fight alone. Comfortable for two.
EliteA small-group encounter — three or four of you.
ApexA major roaming or lair encounter for a full party of six.

Lineages

Some creatures follow you across the map. The Greenbrush goblins of the Greenfield turn up in the Deep Woodlands as quieter, more patient stalkers — the same people, adapted to different cover. A shared name is worth paying attention to.
