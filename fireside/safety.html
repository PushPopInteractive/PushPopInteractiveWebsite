Suicide and self-harm safety

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (US Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), any time. Outside the US, find a free local line at findahelpline.com. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

You are talking to an AI

Every story and narrator in Fireside is written by artificial intelligence, not a person. A notice under the reply box on every story and narrator screen says all content is AI generated, and the crisis notice says so again.

Fireside is for adults 18 and over. AI companion chats may not be suitable for some minors.

What Fireside does when someone mentions suicide or self-harm

  1. It shows crisis help right away. When a player's own message mentions suicide, self-harm or wanting to die, Fireside shows a notice with buttons to call or text 988 and a link to local lines in other countries. This happens before anything else, whether or not the player is signed in or has Embers.
  2. It tells the AI to step out of the story. The AI is instructed that safety comes before the character, the story and any content setting. If the player may mean it, the AI pauses the story, says plainly that it is an AI, encourages the player to reach 988 or a local line, and offers to keep talking or return to the story later. If the message is clearly part of the fiction, the AI continues with care.
  3. It never gives methods. The AI is instructed never to describe methods, means or instructions for suicide or self-harm, and never to encourage or romanticise it, in any story or setting.

The check is a fixed list of words and phrases, so it can also appear during dark fiction. We would rather show help that isn't needed than miss someone who needs it.

Privacy

Fireside keeps only an anonymous count of how many times the crisis notice was triggered each day. It does not record who triggered it or what they wrote. We use the count for the yearly report California requires.
